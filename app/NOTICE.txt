WHAT THE PROGRAM IS
-------------------

A design aid for venue technology teams: it crosses a venue's design drawings
with its technology equipment request (TEAP) to study where network
equipment rooms (NERs) go and which spaces each one serves. It does not
replace engineering approval, a site survey, cable route validation or
physical inspection. It runs on the user's own computer, sends nothing to the
cloud and keeps the user's documents on that computer.

This is an independent project, built from the author's own experience. It is
not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any event organiser, rights
holder, sports federation, venue owner or employer. It contains no document
belonging to any of them: reference material a team is entitled to (lists of
space names, a naming structure) is kept out of the published source, in
backend/app/reference_private/, and added only to that team's own copies.
Names of products and organisations that may appear in a user's own
documents belong to their owners.
